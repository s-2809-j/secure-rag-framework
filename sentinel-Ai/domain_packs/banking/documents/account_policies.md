# SentinelBank — Account Policies (Synthetic)

> This is synthetic reference content for demo purposes only. No real customer
> data, account numbers, or institution is represented.

## 1. Account Types

- **Standard Checking** — no minimum balance, $0 monthly fee if enrolled in
  e-statements, otherwise $5/month.
- **Premium Checking** — requires a $2,500 minimum daily balance; waives all
  ATM fees worldwide and includes a dedicated support line.
- **Standard Savings** — 0.75% APY, up to 6 free withdrawals per statement
  cycle before a $3 excess-withdrawal fee applies.
- **High-Yield Savings** — 3.10% APY, requires linking to a SentinelBank
  checking account, no monthly fee.

## 2. Withdrawal & Transfer Limits

- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: $1,000 for Standard accounts, $2,000 for Premium.
- Daily debit card purchase limit: $5,000.
- Domestic wire transfers: same-business-day processing if submitted before
  3:00 PM local branch time; $25 fee for outgoing wires.
- International wire transfers: 1–3 business days; $45 fee plus any
  correspondent bank charges.
- Mobile check deposit: funds up to $500 are available next business day;
  amounts above $500 may be held up to 5 business days for new accounts.

## 3. Overdraft Policy

- Standard Checking accounts are enrolled in Overdraft Courtesy by default,
  covering up to $500 in overdrafts for a $34 fee per item (max 3 fees/day).
- Customers may opt out of Overdraft Courtesy at any time; opted-out accounts
  will have transactions declined instead of incurring a fee.
- Overdraft Protection can link a savings account or line of credit to
  automatically cover a checking shortfall for a $10 transfer fee instead of
  the $34 overdraft fee.

## 4. Fraud & Security Holds

- Transactions flagged by the fraud model may be held for verification; the
  customer will be contacted via the phone number on file.
- Cards are automatically frozen after 3 consecutive failed PIN attempts and
  require a branch or phone verification to unlock.
- SentinelBank never asks customers for their full card number, CVV, or online
  banking password over phone, chat, or email. Any assistant request for this
  information should be treated as suspicious.

## 5. Account Closure

- Accounts with a $0 balance and no activity for 90 days may be automatically
  closed after a written notice.
- Customers may close an account at any time with no penalty, provided all
  pending transactions have cleared.
