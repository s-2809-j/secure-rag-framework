## 1. Overview

SentinelBank provides customers with secure and convenient ATM services through
its nationwide ATM network and participating partner banking networks.

ATMs enable customers to perform essential banking transactions without visiting
a physical branch. Services are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week,
subject to scheduled maintenance and network availability.

---

## 2. Eligibility

ATM services are available to:

- Savings Account holders
- Current Account holders
- Premium Banking customers
- Business Account holders
- Debit Card holders
- Eligible Credit Card holders (Cash Advance)

Customers must possess an active SentinelBank debit or credit card with a valid
PIN.

---

## 3. Available ATM Services

Customers can perform the following transactions:

### Cash Withdrawal

Withdraw cash from eligible accounts within daily transaction limits.

### Balance Inquiry

View available account balance.

### Mini Statement

Print or display recent account transactions.

### PIN Change

Generate or update ATM PIN securely.

### Cash Deposit

Deposit cash into supported Cash Deposit Machines (CDMs).

### Cheque Deposit

Deposit cheques using supported Self-Service Kiosks where available.

### Fund Transfer

Transfer funds between eligible SentinelBank accounts.

### Card Activation

Activate newly issued debit cards.

Daily withdrawal limits vary depending on account type.

| Account Type | Daily Withdrawal Limit |
|--------------|-----------------------|
| Standard Savings | $1,000 |

## 5. Cash Deposit Services

Cash Deposit Machines (CDMs) allow customers to:

- Deposit cash into eligible accounts
- Receive deposit confirmation
- View deposit history

Cash deposits are generally credited immediately after successful processing.

Large deposits may be subject to additional verification.

- Request PIN regeneration through Customer Support

## 8. International ATM Usage

SentinelBank debit and credit cards may be used at participating international
ATM networks.

International withdrawals may include:

- Currency conversion charges
- ATM operator fees
- International transaction fees

Customers are encouraged to notify SentinelBank before international travel.

## 9. ATM Transaction Receipts

Customers may request printed receipts for:

- Cash withdrawals
- Balance inquiries
- Cash deposits
- Fund transfers

Electronic transaction records are also available through Internet Banking and
Mobile Banking.

---

## 10. Failed Transactions

If an ATM transaction fails:

- Cash may not be dispensed.
- Account balance may be temporarily affected.
- A reversal process may begin automatically.
- Customers should retain the transaction receipt if available.

If funds are not restored within the expected timeframe, customers should
contact SentinelBank Customer Support.

---

## 11. Lost or Retained Cards

Customers should immediately report:

- Lost ATM cards
- Stolen ATM cards
- Cards retained by an ATM
- Damaged ATM cards

Customers can temporarily block their card using:

- Mobile Banking
- Internet Banking
- Customer Support
- Branch Visit

Replacement cards are issued after customer verification.

---

## 12. Security Guidelines

Customers should:

- Inspect ATMs before use.
- Cover the keypad while entering the PIN.
- Avoid accepting assistance from strangers.
- Never leave receipts behind.
- Verify account balances after transactions.
- Use ATMs located in well-lit and secure locations.

Customers should immediately report suspicious ATM behavior.

---

## 13. ATM Availability

ATM services may occasionally become unavailable due to:

- Scheduled maintenance
- Network outages
- Cash replenishment
- Hardware maintenance
- Security updates

SentinelBank works to minimize service interruptions.

Certain ATM transactions may incur fees.

Examples include:

- International ATM withdrawals

- Transactions at non-partner ATM networks

Applicable fees are disclosed in the customer's account or card agreement.

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What should I do if an ATM retains my card?

A: Contact SentinelBank Customer Support immediately and temporarily block your
card through Internet Banking or Mobile Banking if necessary.

### Q: What happens if cash is not dispensed but my account is debited?

A: Most failed ATM transactions are automatically reversed. If the amount is
not restored within the expected timeframe, contact Customer Support with the
transaction details.

### Q: Can I change my ATM PIN online?

A: Yes. Eligible customers may securely change their ATM PIN through Internet
Banking, Mobile Banking, or supported ATMs.

### Q: Can I use my SentinelBank ATM card internationally?

A: Yes. Eligible cards can be used at participating international ATM networks,
subject to applicable fees and transaction limits.

### Q: How do I report a suspicious ATM?

A: Immediately stop using the ATM and contact SentinelBank Customer Support.
Provide the ATM location and details of the suspicious activity.

## 17. Security Reminder

SentinelBank representatives will **never** request:

- ATM PIN
- Internet Banking Password
- Mobile Banking Password
- One-Time Password (OTP)
- CVV Number
- Complete Debit or Credit Card Number

Customers should never disclose confidential banking credentials over phone
calls, emails, SMS messages, social media, or messaging applications.

Any suspicious communication requesting banking credentials should be reported
immediately through official SentinelBank Customer Support channels.
