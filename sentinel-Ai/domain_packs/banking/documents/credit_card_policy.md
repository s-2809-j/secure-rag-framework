## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I increase my credit limit?

A: Customers may request a credit limit review. Approval depends on income,
credit history, repayment behavior, and internal risk assessment.

### Q: What happens if I miss my payment due date?

A: Late payment fees and interest charges may apply. Repeated missed payments
may affect your credit score.

### Q: How do I block a lost credit card?

A: Customers can instantly block their card using the Mobile Banking
application, Internet Banking portal, or by contacting Customer Support.

### Q: Can I use my credit card outside my home country?

A: Yes, provided international transactions are enabled and the card supports
international usage.

### Q: How are reward points calculated?

A: Reward points are earned based on eligible purchases according to the reward
program applicable to your card type.

## 18. Security Reminder

SentinelBank will **never** request the following information through phone,
email, SMS, or chat:

- Online banking password
- Credit card PIN
- One-Time Password (OTP)
- CVV number
- Complete credit card number

Customers should never disclose these details to anyone claiming to represent
SentinelBank. Any suspicious communication should be reported immediately
through official customer support channels.
