## 1. Overview

SentinelBank is committed to providing reliable, professional, and timely
customer support across all banking products and services. Customer Support
assists customers with account inquiries, transaction disputes, digital banking
issues, card management, loan inquiries, fraud reporting, and general banking
services.

Support services are delivered through secure and authenticated communication
channels to ensure customer privacy and account security.

Customers may contact SentinelBank using the following official support

- Internet Banking Secure Messages

## 4. Customer Verification

Before discussing account information, Customer Support verifies customer
identity.

Verification may include:

- Customer ID
- Registered mobile number
- Date of birth
- Account verification questions
- One-Time Password (OTP), where applicable

Support representatives will never request confidential credentials such as
passwords, PINs, or CVV numbers.

1. Complaint registration
2. Customer identity verification
3. Issue categorization
4. Investigation
5. Resolution
6. Customer notification
7. Case closure

Certain complaints may require coordination with other banking departments.

## 15. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How can I contact SentinelBank Customer Support?

A: Customers can contact support through the official helpline, Internet
Banking, Mobile Banking, secure email, live chat, or by visiting a branch.

### Q: How do I block my debit or credit card?

A: Cards may be blocked immediately through Mobile Banking, Internet Banking,
or by contacting Customer Support.

### Q: Can Customer Support reset my Internet Banking password?

A: Support representatives can guide customers through the password reset
process but cannot view or manually retrieve customer passwords.

### Q: How can I track my complaint?

A: Customers receive a complaint reference number that can be used to check the
status of the investigation.

### Q: How do I report unauthorized transactions?

A: Contact Customer Support immediately using official banking channels and
provide the transaction details for investigation.

## 16. Security Reminder

For customer safety, SentinelBank Customer Support representatives will **never**
ask customers to disclose:

- Internet Banking Password
- Mobile Banking Password
- One-Time Password (OTP)
- Debit Card PIN
- Credit Card PIN
- CVV Number
- Full Debit or Credit Card Number

Customers should never share confidential banking credentials over phone calls,
emails, SMS messages, chat applications, or social media.

Any individual requesting such information while claiming to represent
SentinelBank should be considered fraudulent and reported immediately through
official customer support channels.
