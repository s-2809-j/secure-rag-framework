## 1. Overview

SentinelBank is committed to protecting customers, employees, and financial
assets from fraud and cyber threats. The bank continuously monitors
transactions, account activities, and digital channels to detect suspicious
behavior and prevent unauthorized access.

Customers are encouraged to follow safe banking practices and immediately report
any suspicious activity related to their accounts.

---

## 2. Common Types of Banking Fraud

The following are the most common fraud scenarios observed in digital banking:

- Phishing Emails
- SMS Phishing (Smishing)
- Voice Call Fraud (Vishing)
- Fake Banking Websites
- QR Code Payment Fraud
- Debit and Credit Card Skimming
- Identity Theft
- Account Takeover
- Fake Loan Offers
- Investment Scams
- Business Email Compromise (BEC)
- Social Engineering Attacks

---

## 3. Phishing

Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals impersonate trusted organizations to
obtain confidential information.

Customers should be cautious of:

- Emails requesting login credentials.
- Messages asking for OTPs.
- Links directing users to unofficial banking websites.
- Urgent payment requests claiming account suspension.
- Attachments from unknown senders.

Customers should always verify the sender before responding.

---

## 4. Smishing (SMS Fraud)

Fraudsters may send SMS messages claiming to represent SentinelBank.

Examples include:

- Fake KYC update notifications.
- Reward redemption messages.
- Account suspension alerts.
- Fake loan approval offers.
- Suspicious payment confirmation requests.

Customers should never click unknown links received through SMS.

---

## 5. Vishing (Voice Call Fraud)

Fraudsters may impersonate bank employees over phone calls.

SentinelBank representatives will **never** ask customers to disclose:

- Online banking passwords
- Debit card PIN
- Credit card PIN
- One-Time Password (OTP)
- CVV number
- Full debit or credit card number

Customers should disconnect suspicious calls immediately.

---

## 6. QR Code Fraud

Customers should understand that scanning a QR code **receives or sends money
depending on the payment application being used**.

Fraudsters often trick customers into scanning fake QR codes claiming to:

- Receive refunds.
- Claim cashback rewards.
- Verify KYC.
- Accept prize money.
- Complete account verification.

Customers should only scan QR codes from trusted merchants.

---

## 7. Card Fraud

Card fraud may occur through:

- Card skimming devices.
- Hidden cameras.
- Compromised payment terminals.
- Lost or stolen cards.
- Online merchant data breaches.

Customers should:

- Cover the keypad while entering their PIN.
- Regularly review account statements.
- Enable transaction alerts.
- Report lost cards immediately.

---

## 8. Online Banking Security

To maintain account security, customers should:

- Use strong passwords.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) whenever available.
- Avoid logging in from public computers.
- Keep operating systems and browsers updated.
- Install trusted antivirus software.
- Log out after every banking session.

---

## 9. Password Policy

Customers should create passwords that:

- Are at least 12 characters long.
- Contain uppercase and lowercase letters.
- Include numbers.
- Include special characters.
- Are unique for online banking.

Customers should never reuse passwords from other websites.

Passwords should never be shared with anyone.

---

## 10. OTP Security

One-Time Passwords (OTPs) are used to verify sensitive banking transactions.

Customers must:

- Never share OTPs.
- Never forward OTP messages.
- Ignore requests claiming OTP verification is required by bank staff.
- Contact customer support if OTPs are received unexpectedly.

---

## 11. Suspicious Transactions

Customers should immediately report:

- Unknown account transfers.
- Unauthorized ATM withdrawals.
- Unexpected online purchases.
- Duplicate transactions.
- Failed login notifications.
- Changes to registered contact information.

Prompt reporting increases the likelihood of successful fraud investigation.

## 12. Fraud Detection Process

SentinelBank uses multiple fraud detection techniques, including:

- Transaction monitoring.
- Device fingerprint analysis.
- Geographic anomaly detection.
- Velocity monitoring.
- Login behavior analysis.
- Risk-based authentication.
- Machine learning fraud detection models.

Transactions identified as high risk may be temporarily held for manual review.

## 13. Account Protection Measures

SentinelBank automatically applies several security controls:

- Temporary account lock after multiple failed login attempts.
- Card blocking after repeated incorrect PIN entries.
- Real-time transaction monitoring.
- Suspicious login alerts.
- Device verification for new devices.
- High-value transaction verification.

These measures help reduce unauthorized account access.

## 14. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- Keeping contact information updated.
- Reviewing account activity regularly.
- Reporting lost or stolen cards immediately.
- Protecting login credentials.
- Avoiding unofficial banking applications.
- Updating passwords periodically.

Failure to follow recommended security practices may increase fraud risk.

## 15. Reporting Fraud

Customers can report suspected fraud through:

- 24×7 Customer Support
- Mobile Banking Application
- Internet Banking Portal
- Branch Visit
- Official Email Support

When reporting fraud, customers should provide:

- Customer ID
- Transaction reference (if available)
- Date and time of suspicious activity
- Description of the incident

Additional verification may be required before investigation begins.

---

## 16. Investigation Process

Once a fraud report is received, SentinelBank follows these steps:

1. Register the fraud complaint.
2. Verify customer identity.
3. Freeze affected cards or accounts if necessary.
4. Review transaction history.
5. Conduct fraud investigation.
6. Coordinate with payment networks where applicable.
7. Notify the customer of investigation outcomes.

Investigation timelines vary depending on the complexity of the case.

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What should I do if I receive an unexpected OTP?

A: Do not share the OTP with anyone. If you are not attempting a transaction,
contact SentinelBank immediately.

### Q: How do I block my debit card?

A: Customers can block their card through Internet Banking, the Mobile Banking
application, or by contacting customer support.

### Q: Will SentinelBank ever ask for my banking password?

A: No. SentinelBank employees will never request passwords, PINs, OTPs, or CVV
numbers.

### Q: What should I do if I clicked on a suspicious banking link?

A: Immediately change your online banking password, review recent account
activity, and contact SentinelBank Customer Support.

### Q: Can suspicious transactions be reversed?

A: Some unauthorized transactions may be recoverable depending on investigation
results and applicable banking regulations.
