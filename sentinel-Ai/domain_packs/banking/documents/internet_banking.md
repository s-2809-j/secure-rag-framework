## 1. Overview

SentinelBank Internet Banking provides customers with secure online access to
banking services through a web browser. Customers can manage accounts, transfer
funds, pay bills, monitor transactions, and access various banking services
without visiting a physical branch.

Internet Banking services are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, except
during scheduled maintenance periods.

---

## 2. Eligibility

Internet Banking is available to:

- Savings Account holders
- Current Account holders
- Business Account holders
- Joint Account holders (subject to account permissions)
- Loan customers
- Credit Card customers

Customers must complete KYC verification before Internet Banking access is
activated.

---

## 3. Registration Process

Customers can register by:

- Visiting a SentinelBank branch.
- Registering through the official SentinelBank website.
- Using Mobile Banking (for eligible customers).

Registration requires:

- Customer ID
- Registered mobile number
- Registered email address
- Identity verification
- Successful OTP verification

After successful verification, customers receive Internet Banking credentials.

---

## 4. Login Requirements

Customers require the following credentials:

- Customer ID or Username
- Password
- One-Time Password (OTP), where applicable

SentinelBank recommends enabling Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for enhanced
account security.

---

## 5. Available Services

Internet Banking allows customers to:

- View account balances
- View transaction history
- Download account statements
- Transfer funds
- Pay utility bills
- Manage beneficiaries
- Apply for loans
- Open Fixed Deposits
- Pay Credit Card bills
- Request cheque books
- Block debit or credit cards
- Update selected customer information

Additional services may become available based on account eligibility.

## 6. Fund Transfers

Customers may perform:

### Internal Transfers

Transfer funds between SentinelBank accounts.

### Domestic Transfers

Supported payment methods include:

- NEFT
- RTGS
- IMPS

### Scheduled Transfers

Customers may schedule:

- One-time future payments
- Weekly transfers
- Monthly transfers
- Standing instructions

Transfer limits are determined by customer profile and security settings.

## 7. Beneficiary Management

Before transferring funds to another bank account, customers must register the
beneficiary.

Beneficiary registration requires:

- Account Number
- Bank Name
- Branch Name
- IFSC/SWIFT Code (where applicable)
- Beneficiary Name

Certain beneficiaries may require a cooling period before becoming active.

## 8. Account Statements

Customers may:

- View transaction history
- Download PDF statements
- Export CSV statements
- View monthly summaries
- Search transactions
- Filter by date range

Historical statements are available according to SentinelBank's record retention
policy.

## 9. Password Management

Customers should create passwords that:

- Contain at least 12 characters
- Include uppercase letters
- Include lowercase letters
- Include numbers
- Include special characters

Passwords should:

- Never be shared
- Never be reused across websites
- Be updated periodically

Customers should avoid predictable passwords such as birthdays or names.

---

## 10. Session Management

For customer protection:

- Sessions automatically expire after periods of inactivity.
- Multiple simultaneous logins may require additional verification.
- Customers should always log out after completing banking activities.
- Browser sessions should not be left unattended.

---

## 11. Security Features

SentinelBank Internet Banking includes:

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- OTP verification
- Encrypted communication
- Device recognition
- Login alerts
- Transaction alerts
- Fraud monitoring
- Automatic session timeout
- Risk-based authentication

These controls help protect customer accounts from unauthorized access.

---

## 12. Failed Login Attempts

If multiple unsuccessful login attempts occur:

- The account may be temporarily locked.
- Additional identity verification may be required.
- Password reset may be necessary.
- Customer Support may need to verify account ownership.

This security measure helps prevent unauthorized login attempts.

---

## 13. Password Reset

Customers may reset passwords using:

- Registered mobile number
- Registered email
- OTP verification
- Identity confirmation

For security reasons, customer support representatives cannot view customer
passwords.

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I access Internet Banking from another country?

A: Yes. Customers may access Internet Banking internationally provided internet
access is available and additional security verification is completed when
required.

### Q: What should I do if I forget my password?

A: Use the "Forgot Password" option on the official SentinelBank login page and
complete OTP verification to create a new password.

### Q: Why was my Internet Banking account locked?

A: Accounts may be temporarily locked after repeated unsuccessful login
attempts or suspicious login activity.

### Q: Can I download my account statements?

A: Yes. Customers can download account statements in PDF format and export
transaction history where supported.

### Q: How can I report unauthorized Internet Banking activity?

A: Immediately contact SentinelBank Customer Support or temporarily block
Internet Banking access through available security options.

---

## 18. Security Reminder

Customers should always verify that they are using the official SentinelBank
website before entering login credentials.

SentinelBank will **never** ask customers through email, SMS, phone calls, or
chat to disclose:

- Internet Banking password
- One-Time Password (OTP)
- Debit Card PIN
- Credit Card PIN
- CVV number
- Complete card details

Customers should immediately report any suspicious emails, websites, phone
calls, or login requests claiming to represent SentinelBank.
