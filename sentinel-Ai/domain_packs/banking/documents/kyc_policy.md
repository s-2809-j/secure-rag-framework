## 1. Overview

Know Your Customer (KYC) is a mandatory customer identification and verification
process followed by SentinelBank before providing banking products and
financial services.

The objective of KYC is to:

- Verify customer identity.
- Prevent identity fraud.
- Reduce money laundering risks.
- Prevent terrorist financing.
- Comply with applicable financial regulations.
- Protect customer accounts from unauthorized access.

Every customer must successfully complete KYC before opening or operating
certain banking products.

## 2. KYC Objectives

The KYC program is designed to:

- Establish customer identity.
- Verify residential address.
- Understand customer financial profile.
- Assess customer risk level.
- Monitor suspicious financial activity.
- Maintain updated customer records.

SentinelBank periodically reviews customer information to ensure accuracy.

SentinelBank supports the following verification methods.

### Full KYC

Full KYC includes complete identity verification using official documents.

Suitable for:

### Simplified KYC

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why is KYC required?

A: KYC helps verify customer identity, prevent fraud, comply with financial
regulations, and protect banking services from misuse.

### Q: Can I open an account without completing KYC?

A: Some basic banking products may allow limited functionality until KYC is
completed. Full banking services require successful KYC verification.

### Q: How long does KYC verification take?

A: Most applications are processed within 1–3 business days after all required
documents are submitted.

### Q: Can I update my KYC details online?

A: Eligible customers may update selected KYC information through Internet
Banking or Mobile Banking. Certain changes may require branch verification.

### Q: What happens if my KYC expires?

A: Customers may experience temporary restrictions on selected banking services
until updated KYC documentation is submitted and verified.

## 17. Security Reminder

SentinelBank representatives will **never** request customers to send identity
