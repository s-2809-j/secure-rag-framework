## 1. Overview

SentinelBank offers a range of lending products designed to meet the financial
needs of individuals and businesses. Loan applications are evaluated based on
customer eligibility, repayment capacity, credit history, and applicable
banking regulations.

The bank reserves the right to approve, reject, or request additional
documentation for any loan application.

---

## 2. Loan Products

SentinelBank currently offers the following loan products:

- **Personal Loan** – For personal expenses, medical emergencies, education,
  travel, or home renovation.
- **Home Loan** – For purchasing, constructing, or renovating residential
  property.
- **Vehicle Loan** – For purchasing new or pre-owned cars and two-wheelers.
- **Education Loan** – For higher education in domestic and international
  institutions.
- **Business Loan** – For business expansion, equipment purchases, and working
  capital requirements.
- **Loan Against Fixed Deposit** – Secured loan against eligible fixed deposits
  maintained with SentinelBank.

## 3. General Eligibility Criteria

Applicants should satisfy the following minimum requirements:

- Minimum age: **21 years**
- Maximum age at loan maturity: **65 years**
- Applicant must possess a valid government-issued identity proof.
- Applicant must have an active SentinelBank account or complete customer
  onboarding during loan processing.
- A satisfactory credit history is preferred.
- Income and employment stability are evaluated during loan assessment.

Additional eligibility requirements may apply depending on the loan product.

## 10. Late Payment Policy

Failure to make timely repayments may result in:

- Late payment fees
- Additional interest charges
- Credit score impact
- Temporary suspension of additional lending services
- Collection procedures in accordance with applicable regulations

Repeated missed payments may result in loan default proceedings.

## 11. Prepayment and Foreclosure

Customers may repay loans before maturity subject to product-specific terms.

Some loan products may include:

- No prepayment charges after a specified lock-in period.
- Partial prepayments without penalty up to permitted annual limits.
- Foreclosure charges for selected commercial lending products.

Applicable charges, if any, are disclosed during loan agreement signing.

## 15. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I apply for multiple loans simultaneously?

A: Yes. Each application is evaluated independently based on eligibility,
repayment capacity, and existing financial obligations.

### Q: How long does loan approval usually take?

A: Most complete applications are processed within 2–7 business days. Complex
applications may require additional verification.

### Q: Can I repay my loan early?

A: Yes. Early repayment options depend on the specific loan product and the
terms of your loan agreement.

### Q: What happens if my application is rejected?

A: Customers may reapply after addressing the reasons for rejection or after
their financial circumstances have changed.

### Q: Does SentinelBank guarantee loan approval?

A: No. Approval depends on successful completion of identity verification,
credit assessment, document validation, and internal risk evaluation.

## 16. Security Reminder

SentinelBank representatives will **never** ask customers to disclose:

- Online banking passwords

- Complete debit or credit card numbers
