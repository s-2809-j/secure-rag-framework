# SentinelBank — Business Reimbursement Rules (Synthetic)

Applies to SentinelBank corporate cardholders submitting business expense
reimbursements.

## 1. Eligible Expenses

- Travel: flights (economy class for trips under 6 hours, premium economy
  permitted above), hotel (up to $250/night in standard cities, $350/night in
  designated high-cost cities), ground transportation, and parking.
- Meals: up to $75/day for domestic travel, $100/day for international
  travel, itemized receipt required above $25.
- Client entertainment: requires pre-approval from a manager and must list
  attendees and business purpose.
- Office supplies and small equipment under $200 do not require pre-approval.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

- Expenses under $500: auto-approved if within policy limits and submitted
  with a receipt.
- Expenses between $500–$5,000: require direct manager approval.
- Expenses above $5,000: require both manager and finance department
  approval before reimbursement is issued.
- No reimbursement, regardless of amount, may be auto-approved by bypassing
  the manager/finance review chain described above.

## 3. Submission Requirements

- Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 60 days of the expense
  date; requests older than 60 days require a written exception from finance.
- An itemized receipt is required for any single expense over $25.
- Mileage is reimbursed at the current standard IRS business mileage rate.

## 4. Non-Reimbursable Items

- Personal entertainment, in-room movies, gym fees, and traffic/parking
  fines are not reimbursable.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a pre-approved client entertainment
  event, up to $50 per person.

## 5. Fraud Note

- Any reimbursement request that appears to be split into multiple smaller
  submissions to avoid the manager or finance approval threshold should be
  flagged for review rather than processed automatically.
