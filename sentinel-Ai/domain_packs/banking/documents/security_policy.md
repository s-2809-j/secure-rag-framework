## 1. Overview

SentinelBank is committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and
availability of customer information, banking systems, and digital services.
The Information Security Policy establishes the security standards and best
practices followed by SentinelBank to safeguard its digital infrastructure,
employees, customers, and financial assets.

Security controls are continuously reviewed and improved to address emerging
cybersecurity threats.

## 2. Security Objectives

The primary objectives of SentinelBank's Information Security Program are to:

- Protect customer information.
- Prevent unauthorized system access.
- Ensure continuous availability of banking services.
- Detect and respond to security incidents.
- Comply with regulatory and legal requirements.
- Minimize cybersecurity risks.

SentinelBank follows these core security principles:

- Confidentiality

- Availability
- Least Privilege
- Zero Trust
- Defense in Depth
- Secure by Design
- Continuous Monitoring

## 4. Identity and Access Management

Access to banking systems is granted according to business responsibilities.

Access controls include:

- Unique user accounts
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Password policies
- Session monitoring
- Periodic access reviews

Employees receive only the minimum permissions required for their duties.

---

## 5. Password Policy

Passwords should:

- Contain at least 12 characters.
- Include uppercase letters.
- Include lowercase letters.
- Include numbers.
- Include special characters.

Passwords must never:

- Be shared.
- Be written in publicly accessible locations.
- Be reused across multiple systems.
- Contain easily guessed information.

Passwords should be updated periodically according to organizational guidelines.

## 19. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Does SentinelBank store customer passwords?

A: Passwords are stored using secure cryptographic techniques. Employees cannot
view customer passwords.

### Q: What should I do if I suspect my account has been compromised?

A: Immediately change your password, block affected cards if necessary, and
contact SentinelBank Customer Support.

### Q: Will SentinelBank ask me for my OTP?

A: No. SentinelBank employees will never request OTPs, passwords, PINs, or CVV
numbers.

### Q: How does SentinelBank protect customer information?

A: Customer information is protected through encryption, access controls,
continuous monitoring, secure authentication, and regulatory compliance.

### Q: What happens if suspicious activity is detected?

A: SentinelBank may temporarily restrict access, contact the customer for
verification, and investigate the activity before restoring normal account
operations.
