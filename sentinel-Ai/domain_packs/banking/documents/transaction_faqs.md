# SentinelBank — Transaction FAQs (Synthetic)

**Q: Why is a pending transaction showing a different amount than what I was
charged?**
A: Merchants like hotels, rental car companies, and gas stations often place a
temporary authorization hold that's higher than the final charge. The hold
should adjust to the final amount within 1–5 business days.

**Q: How do I dispute a transaction I don't recognize?**
A: Log a dispute through the app under Transactions → Dispute, or call
support. Provisional credit is typically issued within 10 business days while
the dispute is investigated, per standard Reg E timelines.

**Q: Can I reverse a wire transfer after it's sent?**
A: Domestic wires generally cannot be reversed once processed unless the
receiving bank agrees to return the funds voluntarily. Contact support
immediately if you believe a wire was sent in error.

**Q: Why was my card declined even though I have funds available?**
A: Common causes: the merchant category is flagged as high-risk, the daily
purchase limit was reached, or the fraud model flagged the transaction pattern
(e.g., a purchase in an unusual location). Contact support to verify and lift
a hold if needed.

**Q: How long do refunds take to appear in my account?**
A: Most merchant refunds post within 3–5 business days after the merchant
initiates them, though the merchant's own processing time varies.

**Q: What's the difference between a "hold" and a "pending" transaction?**
A: A hold reserves funds without a confirmed transaction (e.g., hotel
deposit); a pending transaction is a transaction that has been authorized and
is awaiting final settlement, usually within 1–2 business days.

**Q: Can I set up recurring transfers between my accounts?**
A: Yes, recurring internal transfers can be scheduled daily, weekly, or
monthly through the app with no fee.

**Q: What should I do if I suspect fraudulent activity on my account?**
A: Freeze your card immediately through the app, then contact support. Do not
share your card number, CVV, or online banking credentials with anyone
claiming to be from SentinelBank support — official support will never ask
for these directly.
