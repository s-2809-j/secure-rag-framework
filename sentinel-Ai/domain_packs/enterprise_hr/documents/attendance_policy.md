## 1. Overview

SentinelCorp maintains an attendance management system to ensure operational
efficiency, accountability, and fair workforce management. Employees are
expected to report to work on time, accurately record their attendance, and
follow company attendance procedures.

This policy applies to all eligible employees unless otherwise specified in
their employment agreement.

---

## 2. Purpose

The Attendance Policy aims to:

- Maintain consistent working hours.
- Ensure accurate attendance records.
- Promote workplace discipline.
- Support payroll processing.
- Improve workforce planning.
- Encourage punctuality and accountability.

---

## 3. Working Schedule

The standard working schedule is:

- Monday to Friday
- 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- One-hour lunch break
- Total working hours: 8 hours per day

Departments operating on shifts may follow different schedules approved by
Human Resources.

---

## 4. Attendance Recording

Employees are required to record attendance using approved systems such as:

- Biometric attendance system
- Employee ID card
- HR Portal
- Mobile attendance application
- Approved remote attendance system

Employees should record both check-in and check-out times.

---

## 5. Punctuality

Employees are expected to:

- Arrive before their scheduled start time.
- Begin work promptly.
- Complete assigned working hours.
- Notify managers if delayed.

Consistent punctuality contributes to effective team collaboration and service
delivery.

---

## 6. Late Arrival

An employee is considered late if they report after the scheduled start time
without prior approval.

Occasional delays may be accepted for valid reasons.

Repeated late arrivals may result in:

- Manager counseling
- Attendance review
- Performance discussions
- Corrective action where necessary

---

## 7. Early Departure

Employees wishing to leave before the scheduled end of the workday must obtain
approval from their immediate manager.

Frequent early departures may affect attendance records and performance
evaluations.

---

## 8. Absence from Work

Employees who cannot report to work should:

- Inform their immediate manager.
- Notify Human Resources where applicable.
- Submit leave requests through the HR Portal.
- Provide supporting documentation when required.

Failure to notify the company may be treated as unauthorized absence.

---

## 9. Remote Attendance

Employees approved for remote work must:

- Log in using approved company systems.
- Record attendance through the HR Portal.
- Be available during official working hours.
- Attend scheduled meetings.
- Respond to work-related communications promptly.

Remote employees remain subject to the same attendance expectations as office
employees.

---

## 10. Overtime

Overtime may be performed only after prior approval from the reporting manager.

Eligible overtime may be compensated through:

- Overtime payment
- Compensatory leave
- Alternative arrangements approved by Human Resources

Unauthorized overtime may not qualify for compensation.

---

## 11. Breaks

Employees are entitled to:

- One lunch break
- Short refreshment breaks as permitted by departmental guidelines

Break durations should not interfere with business operations.

---

## 12. Attendance Monitoring

Human Resources monitors attendance records to identify:

- Late arrivals
- Early departures
- Unauthorized absences
- Attendance trends
- Overtime records
- Shift compliance

Attendance reports may be reviewed during performance evaluations.

---

## 13. Attendance Corrections

Employees who believe their attendance record is incorrect should:

1. Notify their manager.
2. Submit an attendance correction request.
3. Provide supporting information.
4. Await HR verification.

Only authorized personnel may modify attendance records.

---

## 14. Unauthorized Absence

Unauthorized absence includes:

- Failure to report for work without notification.
- Leaving the workplace without approval.
- Failure to return after approved leave.
- Repeated attendance violations.

Unauthorized absences may result in disciplinary action.

---

## 15. Shift Employees

Employees assigned to rotational shifts should:

- Follow assigned schedules.
- Arrive before shift handover.
- Complete shift documentation.
- Notify supervisors regarding scheduling conflicts.

Shift assignments are determined by business requirements.

---

## 16. Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for:

- Recording attendance accurately.
- Following assigned work schedules.
- Informing managers of delays.
- Maintaining accurate attendance records.
- Following attendance procedures.
- Cooperating during attendance verification.

Employees should never attempt to manipulate attendance records.

---

## 17. Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Monitoring employee attendance.
- Approving attendance corrections.
- Reviewing overtime requests.
- Addressing attendance concerns promptly.
- Supporting workforce scheduling.

Managers should apply attendance policies fairly and consistently.

---

## 18. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I record my attendance?

A: Employees should use the approved attendance system assigned to their work
location, such as the biometric system or HR Portal.

### Q: What should I do if I am running late?

A: Notify your immediate manager as soon as possible and provide the expected
arrival time.

### Q: Can I correct an incorrect attendance record?

A: Yes. Submit an attendance correction request through the HR Portal with any
required supporting information.

### Q: Does remote work require attendance recording?

A: Yes. Employees working remotely must record attendance using the approved HR
systems.

### Q: Is overtime automatically approved?

A: No. Overtime generally requires prior approval from the reporting manager.

---

## 19. Security Reminder

Employees should never:

- Share HR Portal credentials.
- Allow others to record attendance on their behalf.
- Manipulate attendance records.
- Access another employee's attendance information.
- Use unauthorized attendance devices or software.

Any suspected attendance fraud or unauthorized access should be reported
immediately to Human Resources or the Information Security Team.
