and respectful workplace. This Code of Conduct establishes the behavioral

Every employee is expected to uphold the company's values and conduct business
with honesty, integrity, and professionalism.

- Foster a respectful and inclusive environment.

- Encourage responsible decision-making.

## 6. Anti-Harassment Policy

Harassment includes any unwelcome behavior that creates an intimidating,
hostile, or offensive work environment.

Examples include:

- Verbal harassment
- Physical harassment
- Sexual harassment
- Bullying
- Threatening behavior
- Offensive jokes
- Repeated unwanted communication

Employees should report harassment immediately to Human Resources.

## 7. Workplace Violence

SentinelCorp has zero tolerance for workplace violence.

Prohibited behavior includes:

- Physical assault
- Threats of violence
- Intimidation
- Possession of prohibited weapons
- Destruction of company property

Any immediate safety concern should be reported to management and security
personnel without delay.

## 8. Conflict of Interest

- Accepting inappropriate gifts.
- Working for competing organizations without approval.
- Using company information for personal gain.
- Hiring close family members without disclosure.

Potential conflicts should be disclosed to management promptly.

## 18. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What should I do if I witness workplace harassment?

A: Report the incident immediately to Human Resources, your manager, or the
Ethics Hotline. Reports will be handled confidentially whenever possible.

### Q: Can I accept gifts from clients?

A: Employees may accept modest gifts that comply with company policy and do not
create conflicts of interest.

### Q: Can I share company information on social media?

A: No. Confidential company information should never be shared publicly unless
authorized.

### Q: What happens if I violate the Code of Conduct?

A: Violations may result in disciplinary action depending on the severity of
the incident.

### Q: Can I report misconduct anonymously?

A: Where permitted by company policy and local regulations, anonymous reports
may be submitted through the Ethics Hotline.
