## 1. Overview

SentinelCorp reimburses employees for reasonable and approved business expenses
incurred while performing official company duties. This policy establishes the
guidelines for submitting, approving, processing, and reimbursing business
expenses while ensuring financial accountability and regulatory compliance.

Employees are expected to spend company funds responsibly and submit accurate,
complete, and timely expense claims.

---

## 2. Purpose

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Standardize expense reimbursement procedures.
- Ensure responsible use of company funds.
- Prevent fraudulent expense claims.
- Maintain accurate financial records.
- Define approval requirements.
- Ensure timely reimbursement processing.

---

## 3. Eligibility

Expense reimbursement is available for:

- Full-Time Employees
- Contract Employees
- Project Consultants
- Business Development Teams
- Senior Management

Interns and temporary employees may claim expenses only when specifically
authorized.

---

## 4. Eligible Business Expenses

The following expenses may qualify for reimbursement:

### Travel Expenses

- Airfare
- Train tickets
- Taxi fares
- Public transportation
- Parking fees
- Toll charges

### Accommodation

- Hotel stays
- Business lodging
- Conference accommodation

### Meals

Reasonable meal expenses incurred during approved business travel.

### Office Expenses

- Office supplies
- Printing
- Courier services
- Business software subscriptions
- Internet charges (where approved)

### Client Meetings

- Business meals
- Meeting room bookings
- Presentation materials

All expenses must directly support business activities.

---

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are generally not reimbursable:

- Personal shopping
- Entertainment unrelated to business
- Alcohol (unless officially approved)
- Traffic fines
- Parking violations
- Family travel expenses
- Personal insurance
- Luxury purchases
- Personal subscriptions
- Vacation expenses

Employees are responsible for all personal expenses.

---

## 6. Expense Approval

Expense approval depends on the total claim amount.

| Expense Amount | Approval Required |
|----------------|------------------|
| Up to $500 | Reporting Manager |
| $501 – $5,000 | Department Manager |
| Above $5,000 | Department Head + Finance Department |

Finance may request additional supporting documentation before approval.

---

## 7. Expense Submission

Employees should submit expense claims through the HR or Finance Portal.

Each claim should include:

- Expense category
- Expense date
- Business purpose
- Amount claimed
- Currency (if applicable)
- Supporting receipts
- Manager approval where required

Incomplete submissions may delay reimbursement.

---

## 8. Receipt Requirements

Receipts should clearly display:

- Merchant name
- Transaction date
- Description of goods or services
- Amount paid
- Currency
- Payment method (where available)

Digital receipts are acceptable if they are complete and clearly legible.

Lost receipts may require additional manager approval.

---

## 9. Submission Timeline

Employees should submit expense claims within:

- **30 calendar days** of the expense date.

Claims submitted after the deadline may require additional approval and may be
rejected if adequate justification is not provided.

---

## 10. Foreign Currency Expenses

Business expenses incurred in foreign currencies should include:

- Original receipt
- Currency used
- Exchange rate (if known)
- Credit card statement (where applicable)

Currency conversion is performed using the company's approved exchange rate for
the transaction date.

---

## 11. Expense Verification

Before reimbursement, expense claims are reviewed to verify:

- Business purpose
- Policy compliance
- Receipt authenticity
- Required approvals
- Duplicate submissions
- Budget availability

Additional clarification may be requested during the verification process.

---

## 12. Fraud Prevention

SentinelCorp actively monitors expense claims to detect fraudulent activities.

Examples include:

- Duplicate receipts
- Altered invoices
- Fake receipts
- Personal expenses claimed as business expenses
- Inflated expense amounts
- Unauthorized purchases

Fraudulent claims may result in:

- Claim rejection
- Internal investigation
- Recovery of reimbursed funds
- Disciplinary action
- Termination of employment in severe cases

---

## 13. Reimbursement Processing

After approval:

1. Expense verification
2. Finance review
3. Payment authorization
4. Reimbursement processing
5. Employee notification

Approved reimbursements are generally credited within **7–10 business days**.

---

## 14. Payment Method

Approved reimbursements are paid through:

- Direct bank transfer
- Payroll reimbursement (where applicable)

Cash reimbursements are generally not provided.

## 18. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What expenses are reimbursable?

A: Approved travel, accommodation, meals, office supplies, client meeting
expenses, and other authorized business expenses.

### Q: How long do reimbursements take?

A: Approved claims are generally processed within 7–10 business days after
final approval.

### Q: Can I submit an expense without a receipt?

A: Receipts are normally required. Missing receipts may require additional
approval and supporting documentation.

### Q: Can personal expenses be reimbursed?

A: No. Only legitimate business-related expenses are eligible.

### Q: What happens if my claim is rejected?

A: Employees will receive a notification explaining the reason and may resubmit
the claim after addressing any identified issues.

---

## 19. Security Reminder

Employees should never:

- Share Finance Portal passwords.
- Share HR Portal credentials.
- Share Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) codes.
- Modify expense records without authorization.
- Submit fraudulent receipts.
- Access another employee's expense claims.

SentinelCorp Finance and Human Resources personnel will **never** request
employee passwords, banking PINs, One-Time Passwords (OTP), or confidential
authentication credentials through email, phone calls, messaging applications,
or social media.

Any suspected reimbursement fraud, unauthorized expense claim, or suspicious
financial activity should be reported immediately to the Finance Department,
Human Resources, or the Information Security Team.
