## 1. Overview

SentinelCorp recognizes the importance of maintaining a healthy work-life
balance. The company provides eligible employees with various types of leave to
support personal well-being, family responsibilities, medical needs, and
professional commitments.

Employees are expected to request leave responsibly and follow the approval
process outlined in this policy.

---

## 2. Eligibility

Leave benefits are available based on employment type.

Eligible employees include:

- Full-Time Employees
- Part-Time Employees (where applicable)
- Contract Employees (as specified in their contract)
- Probationary Employees (limited benefits may apply)

Interns and temporary employees may have different leave entitlements based on
their employment agreement.

---

## 3. Annual Leave

Eligible full-time employees receive:

- **20 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave should be planned in advance to minimize disruption to business
operations.

Managers may approve or reject leave requests based on operational
requirements.

---

## 4. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to:

- **12 days of paid sick leave** per calendar year.

For sick leave exceeding three consecutive working days, employees may be
required to submit a medical certificate issued by a registered healthcare
professional.

The company may request additional documentation where necessary.

---

## 5. Casual Leave

Employees may use casual leave for:

- Personal commitments
- Family events
- Urgent personal matters
- Short-term emergencies

Employees are entitled to:

- **8 days of casual leave** annually.

Casual leave should normally be requested in advance unless an emergency
prevents prior notification.

---

## 6. Maternity Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to paid maternity leave in accordance with
applicable employment laws.

Maternity leave generally includes:

- Paid leave before childbirth
- Paid leave after childbirth
- Additional medical leave where required

Employees should notify Human Resources as early as reasonably possible to
support workforce planning.

---

## 7. Paternity Leave

Eligible employees may receive paid paternity leave following the birth or
adoption of a child.

The duration of leave is determined according to company policy and applicable
employment regulations.

---

## 8. Bereavement Leave

Employees may request bereavement leave following the death of an immediate
family member.

Bereavement leave is intended to provide employees sufficient time to:

- Attend funeral services
- Support family members
- Manage personal responsibilities

Supporting documentation may be requested when appropriate.

---

## 9. Unpaid Leave

Employees who have exhausted paid leave balances may request unpaid leave.

Approval depends on:

- Business requirements
- Employee performance
- Department workload
- Manager approval

Unpaid leave is granted at the discretion of SentinelCorp.

---

## 10. Public Holidays

SentinelCorp observes officially recognized public holidays applicable to the
employee's work location.

Employees required to work on public holidays may receive:

- Compensatory leave
- Overtime compensation
- Alternative holiday arrangements

Subject to company policy and local employment regulations.

---

## 11. Leave Carry Forward

Unused annual leave may be carried forward into the next calendar year,
subject to company policy.

Employees may carry forward a maximum of:

- **10 unused annual leave days**

Unused leave exceeding the permitted limit may expire unless otherwise approved
by Human Resources.

Casual leave and sick leave generally cannot be carried forward.

---

## 12. Leave Application Process

Employees should follow these steps:

1. Submit leave request through the HR Portal.
2. Provide leave dates.
3. Specify leave type.
4. Include supporting documents if required.
5. Obtain manager approval.
6. Receive confirmation from the HR system.

Leave should not be considered approved until official confirmation is received.

---

## 13. Emergency Leave

Employees experiencing emergencies should notify:

- Immediate Manager
- Human Resources

As soon as reasonably possible.

Emergency leave requests are evaluated individually based on the circumstances.

---

## 14. Leave Cancellation

Employees may cancel or modify approved leave before the scheduled start date.

Changes are subject to:

- Manager approval
- Business requirements
- Team workload

Previously approved leave cannot always be reinstated after cancellation.

---

## 15. Leave During Probation

Employees serving a probation period may:

- Use sick leave where applicable.
- Request emergency leave.
- Receive limited annual leave according to employment agreements.

Excessive leave during probation may extend the probation period where permitted
by company policy.

---

## 16. Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for:

- Planning leave responsibly.
- Applying for leave in advance whenever possible.
- Providing accurate information.
- Submitting supporting documentation.
- Returning to work on the approved return date.
- Informing managers about any extension requests.

Failure to follow leave procedures may result in leave being treated as
unauthorized absence.

---

## 17. Unauthorized Absence

Unauthorized absence includes:

- Failure to report to work without approval.
- Failure to notify managers.
- Providing false information.
- Misuse of leave benefits.

Repeated unauthorized absences may result in disciplinary action.

---

## 18. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How many annual leave days do employees receive?

A: Eligible full-time employees receive 20 working days of paid annual leave
per calendar year.

### Q: Can unused annual leave be carried forward?

A: Yes. Employees may carry forward up to 10 unused annual leave days,
subject to company policy.

### Q: Is a medical certificate required for sick leave?

A: A medical certificate may be required when sick leave exceeds three
consecutive working days.

### Q: Can I apply for leave after it has already started?

A: Emergency situations may allow retrospective leave requests, but employees
should notify their manager as soon as possible.

### Q: How do I check my leave balance?

A: Employees can view current leave balances through the SentinelCorp HR
Portal.

---

## 19. Security Reminder

Employees should never:

- Share HR Portal passwords.
- Share Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) codes.
- Modify leave records without authorization.
- Access another employee's leave information.
- Submit fraudulent leave documentation.

Any suspected misuse of the leave management system or unauthorized access to
employee records should be reported immediately to Human Resources or the
Information Security Team.
