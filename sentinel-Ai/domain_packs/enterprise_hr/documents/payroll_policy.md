## 20. Security Reminder

Employees should never:

- Share HR Portal passwords.
- Share payroll login credentials.
- Share Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) codes.
- Disclose bank account passwords or OTPs.
- Access another employee's payroll information.
- Modify payroll records without authorization.

SentinelCorp Human Resources and Finance personnel will **never** request
employee passwords, OTPs, banking PINs, or confidential authentication
credentials through email, phone calls, chat applications, or messaging
platforms.

Any suspected payroll fraud, unauthorized payroll access, or suspicious
communication should be reported immediately to Human Resources or the
Information Security Team.
