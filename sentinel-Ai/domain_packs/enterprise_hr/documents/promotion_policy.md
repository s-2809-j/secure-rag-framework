## 1. Overview

SentinelCorp is committed to recognizing employee performance, professional
growth, leadership potential, and organizational contribution through a fair,
transparent, and merit-based promotion process.

Promotions are intended to support career development while ensuring that
employees possess the knowledge, skills, and competencies required for higher
responsibilities.

---

## 2. Purpose

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Reward high-performing employees.
- Encourage continuous learning.
- Provide equal career growth opportunities.
- Support internal talent development.
- Ensure fairness and transparency in promotion decisions.
- Align employee growth with business objectives.

---

## 3. Eligibility

Employees may be considered for promotion if they satisfy the following
requirements:

- Successful completion of probation.
- Minimum service period of 12 months in the current role.
- Consistent performance ratings.
- Good attendance record.
- Compliance with company policies.
- No active disciplinary actions.

Meeting eligibility requirements does not guarantee promotion.

- Professional behavior
- Achievement of business objectives
- Learning and certification achievements

No single criterion determines promotion eligibility.

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How long must I work before becoming eligible for promotion?

A: Employees are generally eligible after completing at least 12 months in
their current role and satisfying performance requirements.

### Q: Are promotions automatic after one year?

A: No. Promotions depend on business needs, performance, competencies, and
available opportunities.

### Q: Can I apply for internal vacancies?

A: Yes. Eligible employees are encouraged to apply for suitable internal job
opportunities.

### Q: Will promotion always increase salary?

A: Most promotions include compensation adjustments, but salary changes depend
on company compensation policies and the new role.

### Q: What if I am not selected for promotion?

A: Employees receive feedback and are encouraged to continue developing their
skills for future opportunities.

## 18. Security Reminder

Employees should never:

- Access confidential promotion records without authorization.
- Share evaluation documents.
- Modify performance records.
- Attempt to influence promotion decisions through unauthorized means.
- Share HR Portal credentials or Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) codes.

Promotion records, salary revisions, and employee evaluations are confidential
and may only be accessed by authorized Human Resources personnel and designated
management.

Any suspected misuse of promotion records, unauthorized access, or data breach
should be reported immediately to Human Resources or the Information Security
Team.
