## 19. Security Reminder

Candidates and employees should never:

- Share recruitment portal passwords.
- Share One-Time Passwords (OTP).
- Share Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) codes.
- Submit confidential company documents through unofficial channels.
- Respond to unofficial recruitment offers claiming to represent
  SentinelCorp.

Official recruitment communications are sent only through authorized company
email addresses and approved recruitment platforms.

Any suspected recruitment fraud, phishing attempt, fake job offer, or
unauthorized request for confidential information should be reported
immediately to Human Resources or the Information Security Team.
