## 1. Overview

SentinelCorp supports flexible work arrangements where business requirements,
job responsibilities, and operational needs permit. The Remote Work Policy
establishes the eligibility criteria, responsibilities, security requirements,
and operational expectations for employees working outside company premises.

Remote work is considered a business arrangement and not an employee
entitlement. Approval depends on business needs and management discretion.

---

## 2. Purpose

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Support flexible work arrangements.
- Maintain employee productivity.
- Ensure business continuity.
- Protect company information.
- Maintain effective communication.
- Promote work-life balance.

---

## 3. Eligibility

Remote work may be approved for:

- Full-Time Employees
- Project Teams
- Technical Staff
- Business Analysts
- Software Engineers
- Support Teams
- Managers

Eligibility depends on:

- Job responsibilities
- Department requirements
- Employee performance
- Business needs
- Manager approval

Certain positions requiring physical presence may not be eligible.

---

## 4. Remote Work Schedule

Unless otherwise approved:

- Standard working days:
  - Monday to Friday

- Standard working hours:
  - 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Employees are expected to remain available during official business hours.

Flexible schedules may be approved where operationally feasible.

---

## 5. Remote Work Approval

Employees requesting remote work should:

1. Submit a remote work request.
2. Provide business justification.
3. Obtain manager approval.
4. Receive Human Resources confirmation.
5. Complete any required security training.

Remote work should not begin until official approval has been granted.

---

## 6. Work Location

Employees should work from:

- Home office
- Approved remote office
- Company-approved coworking space

Employees should avoid working from:

- Public cafés
- Public libraries
- Shared computers
- Unsecured public locations

The remote work location should provide:

- Reliable internet connectivity
- Quiet working environment
- Safe workspace
- Secure storage for company equipment

## 18. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Who is eligible for remote work?

A: Eligibility depends on job responsibilities, business requirements,
performance, and manager approval.

### Q: Can I work from another city?

A: Employees should obtain prior approval before changing their approved remote
work location.

### Q: Is attendance required while working remotely?

A: Yes. Employees must record attendance through the approved HR systems.

### Q: Can I use my personal laptop?

A: Employees should use company-issued devices whenever possible. Personal
devices may require prior approval and compliance with company security
requirements.

### Q: What should I do if I lose internet connectivity?

A: Notify your reporting manager immediately and use an alternative approved
connection if available.

---

## 19. Security Reminder

Remote employees should never:

- Share VPN credentials.
- Share company passwords.
- Share Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) codes.
- Store confidential company files on personal cloud storage.
- Connect company devices to unsecured public Wi-Fi.
- Allow unauthorized individuals to use company devices.

Any suspected security incident, lost company equipment, unauthorized access,
or confidential information disclosure should be reported immediately to the
Information Security Team and Human Resources.
