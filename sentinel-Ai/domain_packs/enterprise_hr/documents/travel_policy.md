## 1. Overview

SentinelCorp supports official business travel when it is necessary to achieve
organizational objectives, meet clients, attend conferences, conduct training,
or perform project-related activities. This policy establishes the procedures,
responsibilities, and reimbursement guidelines associated with official
business travel.

All business travel should be conducted responsibly while ensuring employee
safety, cost efficiency, and compliance with company policies.

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Standardize business travel procedures.
- Ensure employee safety during travel.
- Control travel-related expenses.
- Define approval requirements.
- Promote responsible use of company resources.
- Ensure compliance with financial and operational policies.

## 5. Travel Authorization

Employees must obtain approval before making travel arrangements.

The approval process includes:

1. Submit a travel request.
2. Provide travel purpose.
3. Specify destination.
4. Estimate travel expenses.
5. Obtain manager approval.
6. Receive final approval from the department head (if required).

Travel bookings should only be made after official approval.

## 18. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Who can approve business travel?

A: Business travel requires approval from the reporting manager and may require
additional departmental approval depending on travel type and estimated cost.

### Q: Can employees choose any hotel?

A: Employees should select reasonably priced accommodations that meet company
guidelines. Luxury hotels require additional approval.

### Q: Are personal travel expenses reimbursed?

A: No. Only approved business-related travel expenses are eligible for
reimbursement.

### Q: Can I extend my business trip for personal reasons?

A: Personal travel extensions may be permitted with prior approval. Additional
personal expenses are the employee's responsibility.

### Q: Do I need to keep travel receipts?

A: Yes. Employees should retain all required receipts to support reimbursement
claims.

## 19. Security Reminder

Employees travelling on behalf of SentinelCorp should never:

- Leave company laptops unattended.
- Share corporate passwords.
- Share Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) codes.
- Connect company devices to untrusted public networks.
- Store confidential company information on unsecured devices.
- Share confidential business information in public places.

Any lost company equipment, suspected data breach, or security incident during
travel should be reported immediately to the Information Security Team and the
employee's reporting manager.
