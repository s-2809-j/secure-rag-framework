ACME Corporation Human Resources Policy

Employee Leave Policy

1. Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave.
2. Sick leave may be taken when supported by appropriate documentation.
3. Employees should submit leave requests through the HR portal.
4. Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.
5. Company information should be handled according to internal security policies.

This document is intended for internal employee reference.
